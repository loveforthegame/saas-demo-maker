# Borrow menu (IDs for every storyboard)

Merged from 9 breakdowns of strong product/launch films (Creatify, pool lady, adilitos, Lily's Pad, jungle, API apps, Katana, motion design, motion-ref). Every borrowable element has an **ID** (H1, W2, M5…). Storyboards cite these IDs in a "Borrowed from" column so each choice can be traced and refined. The "idea" columns are examples from a social-media product: adapt them to yours.


---

## 1. Universal rules (every ref agrees)

1. **The hook is an event, not a title.** Something happens to a person or the world in under 1 s (pratfall, plug-snap, warp, flame flare, meme clip). Brand text rides *on* the event and never replaces it.
2. **Two worlds, hard-cut.** Live-action world ↔ clean product world. A graphic touches the footage only through a few sanctioned bridges: orb/caption, notification bubble, payoff bubble, toggle pill. The UI never sits on a laptop screen in-shot.
3. **Human line → product action → world proof.** Each feature is a loop: the hero says or types a request, the UI does it, and a silent world shot shows the result. This repeats, and it escalates.
4. **The UI is calm and the world carries the energy.** UI sections have few or no hard cuts. They move with a virtual camera (push, whip, blur-through). Chaos and flashes belong to the *output* or the hook, never to the UI.
5. **Impact frames are rare and earned.** 1–3 big devices per film. Everything else is a small repeated *motif* (send burst, scan band), which works as grammar, not as impact. Silence or a loudness dip before a reveal counts as an impact device.
6. **No marketing adjectives on screen.** The copy is prompts (casual, first person, 3–8 words), status lines and one payoff number at most. Outputs prove the claims.
7. **Proof is shown in the world, not in charts.** Queue grows, ticket tower, strangers see the ad, lead bubble pops from a real phone.
8. **Payoff mirrors the hook.** Chaos/work at the start, rest/success in the same place at the end. Best version: a callback to the hook creature or character.
9. **Every ref's end card is weak** (tiny caps, no logo, no URL). Do the opposite: logo + one action line + URL, held ≥1.2 s.
10. **AI face drift is real.** Lock the hero with a character sheet. Wardrobe/props carry continuity as backup.

## 5. IMPACT FRAMES (I)

**Discipline (from all 9):**
- 1–3 *big* impacts per film. Spend them on the **hook**, the **product "turns on" moment** and the **payoff/CTA**.
- UI demo sections get **zero** flashes, shake or kinetic words.
- Small repeated motifs (send burst, scan band, develop tint) are grammar, not impact. Use 2–3 of them max in a 15–20 s ad.
- **Silence is an impact.** A loudness dip of 0.3–1 s before a reveal or punchline (motion design 0:25, Katana 0:35.5, Lily's Pad "Introducing").
- Banned as filler: repeated red-duotone/invert flashes (Katana's weak spot), screen shake (no ref uses it), speed ramps on UI.

| ID | Device | Source | When it's earned |
|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | **In-world light event** (flame flare, lightning, neon buzz) instead of a graphic flash | adilitos, jungle | Hook line / climax prompt |
| I2 | **Full-screen brand colour flood + logo stroke write-on**, used once | API apps 14.1 | The product "turns on" |
| I3 | **Lights-out**: background white → grey → black in 2 steps for a hero object | API apps 17.5 | Product hero shot |
| I4 | **Black-to-white smash cut** | Creatify 3.37 | Fantasy → product switch |
| I5 | **Background luminance dip** (white → black → white, 0.4 s each way) on the most boring step | Creatify 5.5 | Setup/URL paste becomes dramatic |
| I6 | **OFF→ON toggle flip with an audio drop** | Katana | The transformation |
| I7 | **Outline→fill verdict word** (10 frames total) | Katana 2.18 | One word judgement in the hook |
| I8 | **One-word-per-beat kinetic sentence + one persistent lower-third claim** | Katana 22.6 | Only inside "output" sections |
| I9 | **Action cut on click → body flop** (0.57 s shot) | motion design 0:10.7 | "One click and done" + humour |
| I10 | **Payoff bubble from a real phone + rack focus**, then it retracts into the phone. One number, no confetti | pool lady 1:07.4 | The single result |
| I11 | **Macro insert of the growing progress prop** (0.7–1.3 s) | adilitos | Each step's proof |
| I12 | **Number roll on a video corner** (129K→333K, ease-out 1.4 s), used once | adilitos | Only with real/attributable numbers (promo-film-rules: no unverified numbers) |
| I13 | **CTA slam**: small word + huge wide black-weight word, scale 1.12 + blur 10 → sharp in 0.2 s | adilitos 1:11 | End |

## 6. MOTION GRAPHICS (M): the rebuild library

GSAP recipes: `motion-recipes.md` (same IDs and section numbers). The IDs below are the menu.

**Live ↔ UI bridges**
- **M1 Voice orb + 2-line teleprompter caption.** The current line is 100% white and the next line sits ghosted at ~35%. They swap in 0.25 s. (motion design 7.3, pool lady 7.1).
- **M2 Orb hand-off.** A small glowing element appears over the laptop in the live shot, then a hard cut shows the same element large on white. (adilitos 7.4)
- **M3 Live shot shrinks into an output card.** Corners round 0→24 px and sibling cards slide out. (adilitos 7.9)
- **M4 Output card dives into live footage.** The card scales up and cuts on motion to a tilt-down inside that image. (Lily's Pad 7.5)
- **M5 Glow-match.** A glowing orb on the subject → zoom-blur → becomes a ring on the UI window. (Katana 7.4)
- **M6 Screen ↔ world match.** The subject runs out of the comp inside the UI and enters the live shot in the same direction. (jungle 7.9)
- **M7 In-world notification bubble** composited beside the real phone, lines blur-in. (jungle 7.2, motion design 7.10)

**Prompt / input**
- **M8 Prompt bar with a hot-character trail.** The newest 3–4 chars are accent-coloured and fade to final. (jungle 7.5, Katana 7.8, API apps 7.1 "lime ink")
- **M9 Camera-pan prompt bar.** The bar is ~2.5× wider than the screen, the camera whip-pans along it and lands on send. (pool lady 7.2, motion design 7.2)
- **M10 Send → burst.** The send button blooms, then pixels/particles scatter and become the next scene. Best when the burst is made of the product's own outputs. (API apps 7.3, jungle 7.5)
- **M11 Attachment drop-in.** The tile enters at 1.3× with the cursor attached and settles into its slot. (Lily's Pad 7.2, Katana 7.7)
- **M12 URL → chip collapse.** (Katana 7.10)
- **M13 Synthetic typing that the camera trucks along** instead of wrapping. (Creatify 7.5)

**"AI is working" (pick ONE signature per film and reuse it 2–4×)**
- **M14 Scan band.** A dot-matrix accent band sweeps each row as it is created or renamed. (motion design 7.6)
- **M15 Holographic "develop".** Results arrive 0.1 s apart with an iridescent tint that resolves to true colour in 0.9 s. (Creatify 7.9)
- **M16 Pixel-regenerate swap.** A band of brand-coloured blocks sweeps across a competitor post and re-skins it as the brand's version. (adilitos 7.8)
- **M17 Pixel-glow ring reveal** from the centre outward. (jungle 7.7)
- **M18 Blur-to-sharp generation card.** Grey placeholder + % + progress bar → image un-blurs from 20 px. (Lily's Pad 7.4)
- **M19 Status card with a grey→black streaming sweep**, staccato copy ("Mapped. Five stops. One route."). (jungle 7.6)
- **M20 "Analyzing" scan across a profile grid.** The active photo lifts 1.08× with a glowing border, the others dim. (Katana 7.11)
- **M21 Analysis callouts.** Pills fly in from the edges with 1 px leader lines to points on the content. (adilitos 7.7)

**Output reveals**
- **M22 Grid fill.** Tiles fill one by one, ~120 ms apart, each blurring in, with a counter ticking. (adilitos 7.6)
- **M23 3D curved wall of reels with tag pills.** The camera trucks through it. (pool lady 7.4)
- **M24 Still → video Flip aspect expand**. (Creatify 7.10)
- **M25 Card fly-in to an IG preview** with rotation + motion blur, then straighten. (pool lady 7.6, adilitos 7.11)
- **M26 Rank climb in 3D perspective.** The hero card floats above the list and locks at #1 with one glow outline. (API apps 7.10)
- **M27 Orbit behind the product.** Light = user view, dark = engine. Shows brand kit / caption / schedule wired in. (API apps 7.8)
- **M28 Toggle-on rows** (ads list V01–V05 flip on, 0.12 s stagger). (adilitos 7.11)

**UI camera and transitions**
- **M29 One-take virtual camera.** Every scene change zooms into the element just clicked. Max 3 hard cuts in 15–20 s. (Creatify)
- **M30 Whip-pan scene change** with directional blur only during the move, overshoot settle. (Creatify 7.3)
- **M31 Blur-through z-push between UI cards.** Out: scale 1→1.15 + blur. In: 0.9→1. Card position alternates L/C/R. (pool lady 7.3, adilitos 7.5)
- **M32 Window slides up as an object over a sky BG.** (jungle 7.10)

**Type**
- **M33 Word-stack hook text.** Words 100 ms apart, key noun bold italic, accent colour. (adilitos 7.1)
- **M34 Cursor-built kinetic title** in a selection box with RGB-split letters + corner shapes. (motion design 7.1)
- **M35 Scramble-decode title** with a colour-cycling tag. (API apps hook)
- **M36 Word-append title card.** The line re-centres as words are added; digit slot-roll. (Lily's Pad 7.1)
- **M37 Floating dialogue words** near the speaker's head, max 3 visible, older ones blur. (jungle 7.3)
- **M38 STEP chapter label** that shrinks to a lower block. (adilitos 7.2)

**Motion-ref (user-added, motion only):** ~15 s of pastel 3D scenes + ~9 s of flat 2D kinetic type + a self-drawing logo. The type half rebuilds almost 1:1 in GSAP; the 3D half needs 2.5D stand-ins. 22 techniques total in `motion-ref-graphics.md`. Top picks:
- **M39 Notification burst → floating cards.** 3 cards fan out from the phone in ~210 ms (`back.out(2.2)`), then a hard cut to a tilted view where they float at different depths.
- **M40 Letterbox crush + close-up cursor click.** Bars close in 625 ms, the cursor glides 250 ms, rests 420 ms, the button brightens on press, and there is a hard cut on the press.
- **M41 End-run type kit.** A coloured square crosses a word and recolours it; a giant word (~83% of frame height) slams in with an `expo.out` drift; the payoff word shrinks 4×→1× in 170 ms into the logo lockup.
- **M42 Ring sweep + container spits out contents.** A big accent ring orbits while letters reveal behind a folder, which pops file cards that smear-resolve into a UI strip.
- **M43 Bottom results panel slide-up.** Rises in 170 ms; each word arrives in the accent colour for 1–2 frames, then turns navy.

**Easing palette across refs:** entrances `power3.out` / `expo.out` 0.2–0.4 s; small pops `back.out(1.6–2.5)`; whips `power4.inOut` 0.15–0.25 s with blur peaking mid-move; holds are never frozen (1–5% drift or scale over the hold).

## 8. COPY + AUDIO

- **Copy:** prompts in the user's voice, lowercase-feeling, 3–8 words, one throwaway word allowed ("…. cinematic"). Status lines are staccato ("Mapped. Five stops. One route."). One payoff number max, and it must be real (promo-film-rules). Proper capitalisation on screen.
- **Captions:** 2-line teleprompter (M1) or floating words (M37). Never word-pop karaoke.
- **Audio (loudness patterns only):** quiet 0–1 s, then a hit on the hook event; a dip on every world-proof beat; a dip before reveals and punchlines; music fades under the end card. Katana: OFF = diegetic and quiet, ON = loud track (audio as a second toggle).

## 10. AVOID (merged)

- Copying any ref's signature literally: purple voice orb, lime pixel glow, pixel cat, ChatGPT/Claude/App Store chrome, "I sell X now.", STEP labels as-is, "LOSER" slang. **Borrow the device and change the look** (promo-film-rules §3: never reuse another video's look).
- Third-party UI or logos as the hero (impersonation).
- Unverified numbers (129K→333K, "billion users", #1 ranks) unless they are clearly illustrative and allowed.
- Long UI stretches (9–12 s). Cap at 3–4 s per demo in short ads, with 3–4 UI states max.
- Tiny UI text, tiny all-caps end cards. Zoom UI 1.5–3× for mobile. CTA ≥1.2 s with the logo.
- Repeated flash filler, shake, more than 3 motifs.
- 16:9 letterboxed into 9:16. Generate native to the target aspect.
- Hooks that never pay off (pool lady's fall, Creatify's astronaut). Close the loop at the end.
